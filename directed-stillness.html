Directed Stillness

For the week's hardest question, or the day's intention. About fourteen minutes.

Bring a question, a request, or an intention into stillness and become receptive, not productive. Ask it once. Let it land. Ask it again. Then stop asking and make room. What surfaces may arrive as words, an image, a felt sense, or a sudden knowing. Your only task is to ask, receive, and remember.
